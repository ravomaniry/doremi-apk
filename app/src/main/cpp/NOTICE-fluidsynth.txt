libdoremi_synth.so links this exact FluidSynth revision statically. CMakeLists.txt
downloads that tarball at configure time and builds it with audio drivers and
optional dependencies disabled. This application repository is the corresponding
source required to relink against a modified FluidSynth.

Audio output is Google Oboe (com.google.oboe:oboe:1.11.0). FluidSynth's own Oboe
driver is not compiled in; the app pulls stereo frames with fluid_synth_write_float.
